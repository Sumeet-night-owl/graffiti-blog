Welcome to the Graffiti blog! This is where we'll share what we're building, how teams are using Graffiti, and tips for giving clearer feedback on the web.

## What is Graffiti?

Graffiti is a Chrome extension that lets you leave comments directly on any web page. Instead of describing a problem in a long message ("the button under the second heading, on the left…"), you pin your comment to the exact spot on the page.

- **Pin comments where they belong.** Click a spot on the page and write your note. The pin stays attached to that part of the page.
- **Keep conversations in one place.** Reply in a thread right next to the content you're discussing.
- **Work with your team.** Teammates see the same comments on the same pages, so everyone stays on the same page, literally.
